# Dashboard Executive Summary: MISO Inventory & Supplier Operations

## Dashboard Purpose
Designed for Merch Ops, Inbound Flow, and Replenishment (RP) leadership, this Looker Studio dashboard shifts focus from high-level summaries to **root-cause diagnostics**. It provides real-time visibility into supplier compliance infractions, their direct statistical impact on store fulfillment, and regional distribution bottlenecks.

## Core Visualizations & Metrics
1. **Global Inventory Health Scorecards:** Overall RP Fill Rate (92.45%), Compliance Infraction Rate (52.58%), and Total Partial Fulfillments (3,019).
2. **Supplier Risk Analysis (Pareto Chart):** Highlights repeat vendor offenders causing ASN and ticketing infractions (e.g., Nordstrom Vendor Partner 17).
3. **Diagnostic Breakdown (Stacked Bar Chart):** Visualizes the statistical dependency between compliance failure types and store fulfillment statuses.
4. **Regional Store Performance:** Ranks retail locations by Partial Fulfillment Rates (%) to pinpoint regional distribution friction.
